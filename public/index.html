Choose a video and its matching DJI SRT file. Extract full-resolution JPEGs with GPS EXIF and available height/gimbal XMP tags. All processing stays in your browser.

These are geotagged camera positions, not orthorectified raster pixels. Video stills are re-encoded JPEGs; original clips remain unchanged.

Timing and missing camera-angle settings

SRT time = video time + offset. For an untrimmed matching pair, leave this at 0. A clip trimmed 10 seconds from the start typically needs +10.

Default 0 requires the frame time to be inside a valid GPS cue. Unmatched frames are skipped. GPS is never interpolated.

Defaults to assumed −90° (straight down), as requested. Used only when SRT camera pitch is missing and labelled as an assumption. Clear the field to leave pitch unknown. Aircraft pitch is never used.

Applies only to older GPS(...) subtitles. Bracketed latitude/longitude tags are unambiguous.
